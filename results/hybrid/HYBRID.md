### Why the hybrid MPC blows up (segment 10009, `scripts/diagnose_hybrid.py`)

No solve failed on that segment (failed steps: []). From step 326 the lataccel error exceeds 1 m/s^2 and stays there while the steer is parked on a region boundary: 162 of the 400 scored steps have the steer within 0.001 of one of the tree's steer thresholds (-0.122, 0.309, -0.351, 0.051, -0.830). My reading: the PWA model is discontinuous at its boundaries, the two neighbouring affine models disagree there, and the optimizer settles on a boundary point whose predicted response the real system does not deliver. A continuous PWA model (continuity constraints at the boundaries, or hinging hyperplanes) would test this; I did not build one.

Single-thread time per step (idle machine, 48 steps): mean 597 ms, max 702 ms, against a 100 ms control period. Not real-time. A test run on 5000 segments was not done: at this speed it would take roughly 400 CPU-hours, and the method is not competitive on dev.
